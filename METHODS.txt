TEAM MERGED VIEW (default)

Input: merged.csv supplied by the team. Each row retains its supplied latitude, longitude, acquisition date, confidence, FRP and n_merged. Count each 10 km equal-area cell once per ISO week. We do not apply the archive-derived 2.93 multiplier, a second confidence conversion, or a fire-type filter to the merged product. Its columns do not encode instrument, fire type, or time of day. A merged location is assigned to one cell; constituent raw positions are unavailable. Sum(n_merged) is an input multiplicity, not a count of independent fires. No opportunity masks are supplied. Counts measure observed activity; missing coverage precludes anomaly flags. The first and last weeks may be partial and are excluded from reference bands. A week without merged rows inside the date bounds is counted as zero records only. The historical band uses up to ten prior ISO years, within two weekly slots, after at least three earlier years.

The raw MODIS, raw VIIRS and adjusted MODIS views and their performance metrics are retained from the previous independent archive analysis. Their filtering differs from the team merge. These metrics do not validate the team merge. The map for a merged week displays its dated points. Exact-hour filtering is unavailable, but you can select the hour for contextual weather separately.

Rebuild: run build.py with the earlier Italy archive, then python build_merged.py merged.csv. The packaged files are ready to use without rebuilding.

ITALY BURNING ACTIVITY CALENDAR

Open index.html locally to explore the packaged calendar. Click a week, then Open week on map. Basemap and map library need internet. For optional weather instead start Start Weather Map.cmd on Windows, or python3 weather_server.py in this folder on Mac/Linux. Enter the team Meteomatics credentials; the browser opens a local server. Choose a week and UTC hour. Weather is a separate online request and may be outside the account's historical entitlement.

Input: the supplied Italy FIRMS download. Italy is the geographic selection of that download; we did not independently clip points against an administrative polygon. The original ZIP is the immutable source. The inventory includes all eight CSVs, with first/last detection dates rather than inferred satellite coverage dates. Only standard archive files enter the analysis. Near-real-time files and the additional NOAA-20/21 VIIRS platforms are inventoried separately. Reference: Suomi NPP alone. MODIS: Terra and Aqua combined; platform availability changes over time. Products include collection and version identifiers in the original files.

Metric: distinct 10 km projected cells with at least one qualifying detection, per ISO week (Monday through Sunday UTC). Type 0 is a presumed vegetation fire; includes potential agricultural burning. Exclude other/unknown types. MODIS confidence >=30, VIIRS nominal/high. Thresholds are operational choices, not equivalent detection probabilities. See build.py for the spherical equal-area projection, origin and floor rule. Counts of cells are neither fire counts nor burned area. Zeros inside the record mean zero matching records. Trim incomplete first/last weeks using detection date bounds. Grey means missing product or opportunity, depending on the selected colour mode.

Adjustment: one nonnegative empirical proportional fit through the origin, minimizing squared error in reference weekly counts. Train on full weeks in calendar years 2013-2020, test in 2021-2025. The adjustment estimates reference-equivalent detected-cell counts, not physical fire activity. Large fire weeks influence the coefficient. Pre-overlap estimates assume this relationship transfers to earlier years, including Terra-only periods. Sensor footprint, satellite availability, cloud and observation timing can affect differences. The seasonal band pools weeks within about two ISO slots of the selected week from up to ten prior ISO years, requiring at least three prior years; it describes the detection record only. Historical adjusted ranges before the fitting period are retrospective.

Coverage is missing in the supplied hotspot-only ZIP. No weeks are flagged anomalous in the delivered dataset. To complete coverage, obtain daily per-pixel clear-land/fire and cloud/no-data observation masks from Terra/Aqua MODIS and Suomi NPP VIIRS, matched to the FIRMS product, period and overpasses, plus an Italy land boundary or fixed land-cell mask. Example pilot rule: count a cell as adequately observed when at least half of its Italian land area was observable on at least three distinct days that week. Combine same-day MODIS opportunities before counting days; avoid double counting satellites. Produce coverage.csv with week (Monday YYYY-MM-DD), sensor (M or V), cell (x:y from build.py), and constant study_cells (total eligible Italy grid cells). Include one row per adequately observed cell even when no fire was detected. Missing coverage is unknown. Document thresholds, versions and boundary. Rebuild with Python, numpy and pandas: python build.py Italy.zip coverage.csv. Without coverage.csv rebuild the provided detection-only calendar. Coverage-qualified numerator counts the intersection of detected and adequately observed cells; denominator counts all adequately observed cells. Raw anomaly flags use observed fractions in weeks covering at least 80% of study cells, with at least three earlier comparable years. No adjusted anomaly flag is inferred. These pilot thresholds require sensitivity analysis.

Files: data/weeks.csv includes raw counts, adjusted estimates, historical bands and coverage fields; data/inventory.csv lists the inputs. Year JS files hold qualifying detections and timestamps for the map. The offline calendar includes all standard-processing years represented in the two selected archives.
